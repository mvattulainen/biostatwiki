# BIOSTAT-001 Clinical claim vs statistical estimand strong and weak patterns

## Trusted-source anchor

> Precision in describing a treatment effect of interest is facilitated by constructing the estimand corresponding to a clinical question of interest. The statistical analysis of clinical trial data should be aligned to the estimand.

Source: ich-e9-r1-addendum-estimands-and-sensitivity-analysis-clinical-trials-guideline-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-step-5_en.pdf, lines 71-90.
Confidence score: 80/100 (BM25/get fallback; rerank unavailable in this runtime).

## Strong patterns

- The SAP states the relevant design choice prospectively.
- The SAP links the clinical question to the endpoint, analysis population, and statistical method.
- The SAP gives enough detail for an independent reviewer to reproduce the analysis decision.
- The SAP distinguishes confirmatory claims from exploratory or descriptive analyses.

## Weak patterns observed

- The SAP states an endpoint but not the corresponding estimand or claim.
- The SAP gives an analysis method without assumptions or sensitivity analyses.
- The SAP lists multiple outcomes without a testing hierarchy.
- The SAP relies on operational text instead of reviewer-ready statistical rationale.

## Recommended fixes

- Add a concise subsection addressing clinical claim vs statistical estimand directly.
- Cite the evidence basis for assumptions and thresholds.
- Add sensitivity analyses where assumptions could drive interpretation.
- Make reviewer-facing limitations explicit rather than implicit.

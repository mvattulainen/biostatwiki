# BIOSTAT-007 Sample size

## Question

Is the sample size sufficient for the primary effectiveness endpoint, safety endpoint, and any key precision requirements?

## Short SAP Evidence

The sample size calculation uses 80% power, alpha 0.05, SD 2.0, a 1.2-point VAS difference, correlation adjustment, and 5% attrition to reach 64 patients.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-007-1: PuraBond PROOF Study is supported for sample size because safety precision or assumption basis may be incomplete

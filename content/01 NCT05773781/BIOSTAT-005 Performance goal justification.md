# BIOSTAT-005 Performance goal justification

## Question

If a single-arm study is used, what external data justify the performance goal, and are those data comparable to the planned population and endpoint?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-005 Performance goal justification]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The study is a randomized parallel-group comparison of two operative techniques, not a single-arm performance-goal study.

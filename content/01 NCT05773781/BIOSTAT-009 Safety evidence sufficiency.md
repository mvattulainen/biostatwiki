# BIOSTAT-009 Safety evidence sufficiency

## Question

Is the study large enough and long enough to characterize important adverse events, device deficiencies, and procedure-related risks?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-009 Safety evidence sufficiency]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The protocol lists device risks and secondary complications, but the sample size is driven by pain VAS rather than adverse-event precision.

# BIOSTAT-008 Power assumptions

## Question

Are assumptions about event rates, success rates, standard deviations, dropout, and treatment effect clinically plausible and evidence-based?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-008 Power assumptions]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The power assumptions include SD, target difference, correlation adjustment, and attrition inflation, with references for pain-score assumptions.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-008-1: PuraBond PROOF Study is partially supported for power assumptions because power assumptions may lack cited evidence or sensitivity ranges

**Trusted source evidence:** ich-e-9-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-step-5_en.pdf, lines 894-910. Semantic confidence: High.

> The basis of sample-size estimates should be given. It is important to investigate sensitivity of the sample size estimate to deviations from assumptions, and assumptions should normally be based on published data or earlier trials.

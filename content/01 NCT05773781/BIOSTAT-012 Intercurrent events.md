# BIOSTAT-012 Intercurrent events

## Question

How will deaths, reinterventions, rescue therapy, device explants, crossovers, discontinuations, or alternative treatments be handled in the primary analysis?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-012 Intercurrent events]]

## Short SAP Evidence

Withdrawal and loss-to-follow-up processes are described, with prior data retained and possible continued follow-up for selected components.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-012-1: PuraBond PROOF Study is partially supported for intercurrent events because intercurrent-event strategy is not expressed as an estimand attribute

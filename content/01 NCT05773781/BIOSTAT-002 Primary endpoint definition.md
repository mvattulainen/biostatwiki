# BIOSTAT-002 Primary endpoint definition

## Question

Is the primary endpoint objectively defined, clinically meaningful, measurable, and aligned with the device's intended purpose?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-002 Primary endpoint definition]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The primary outcome is pain measured using VAS, recorded preoperatively and on days 1, 2, 4, 7, 14, and 30.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-002-1: PuraBond PROOF Study is supported for primary endpoint definition because endpoint clinical justification or measurement validity may need more support

**Trusted source evidence:** ich-e-9-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-step-5_en.pdf, lines 300-323. Semantic confidence: High.

# Statistical methods index

## ANCOVA

Used in:
- NCT05773781 - primary post-operative pain score adjusted for pre-surgical pain score.

Review questions:
- Are baseline covariates prespecified?
- Is model assumption checking described?
- Are adjusted estimates and confidence intervals reported?

## Mixed model

Used in:
- NCT04025762 - primary and secondary CGM outcomes, period-adjusted with random site effect.

Review questions:
- Are subject-level correlations handled?
- Are site/operator effects modeled?
- Are missing visits handled under a plausible assumption?

## Descriptive or pilot analysis

Used in:
- NCT03340025 - pilot wound-healing study with planned sample size below powered target.
- NO-NCT-IRAS286913 - indexed protocol excerpt shows endpoints and randomization, but limited analysis detail.

Review questions:
- Are pilot objectives separated from confirmatory claims?
- Are confidence intervals used instead of over-interpreted p-values?
- Is the next-study decision criterion prespecified?

# BIOSTAT-020 Generalizability and real-world evidence

## Question

Do the investigation population, sites, operators, follow-up, and real-world data sources support generalization to the intended users, patients, and use environment?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-020 Generalizability and real-world evidence]]

## Short SAP Evidence

Single-center adult free-flap donor-site patients are enrolled; generalizability is limited by pilot size and setting.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-020-1: Single-Use Negative Pressure Wound Therapy for Free Flap Donor Sites is partially supported for generalizability and real-world evidence because generalizability is only partially justified

### Gap statement

Complete SAP review shows the target population and setting for NPWT donor-site pilot: 20 adult head and neck surgery patients requiring split-thickness skin graft coverage of a free-flap donor site. Generalizability is partly assessable from population, site/operator, and follow-up descriptions, but the SAP/protocol does not fully translate those design constraints into limits on the intended-use claim.

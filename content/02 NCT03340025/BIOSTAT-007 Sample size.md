# BIOSTAT-007 Sample size

## Question

Is the sample size sufficient for the primary effectiveness endpoint, safety endpoint, and any key precision requirements?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-007 Sample size]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The protocol states 44 patients per group would be needed for 80% power, but only 20 total participants are planned.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-007-1: Single-Use Negative Pressure Wound Therapy for Free Flap Donor Sites is contradicted for sample size because safety precision or assumption basis may be incomplete

### Gap statement

Complete SAP review found a sample-size rationale for NPWT donor-site pilot: 44 patients per group would be needed for 80% power assuming 80% graft take, but the protocol plans only 20 total as a pilot study. The effectiveness rationale is the clearest component; safety precision and robustness of key assumptions are less explicit unless separately quantified in the protocol/SAP.

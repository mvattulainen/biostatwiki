# BIOSTAT-010 Multiplicity

## Question

How will type I error be controlled across multiple primary endpoints, co-primary endpoints, secondary endpoints, interim analyses, subgroups, and repeated looks at the data?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-010 Multiplicity]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The protocol has one primary endpoint family but no multiplicity strategy for repeated time points or multiple wound-healing assessments.

### Gap statement

Complete SAP review shows that NPWT donor-site pilot has multiple outcomes or repeated assessments, but no complete confirmatory multiplicity framework equivalent to a prespecified hierarchy or familywise-error strategy. The gap is not absence of endpoints; it is that the planned claims are not fully protected against multiplicity if interpreted confirmatorily.

# BIOSTAT-020 Generalizability and real-world evidence

## Question

Do the investigation population, sites, operators, follow-up, and real-world data sources support generalization to the intended users, patients, and use environment?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-020 Generalizability and real-world evidence]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The population and recruitment pathway are specific to cataract patients at UHP; generalizability depends on that pathway.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-020-1: Vision and balance changes after bilateral implantation of Toric IOLs is partially supported for generalizability and real-world evidence because generalizability is only partially justified

### Gap statement

Complete SAP review shows the target population and setting for Toric IOL protocol: ambulatory adults aged 50 years or above with bilateral cataract and astigmatism greater than 1.0D awaiting intraocular lens replacement surgery. Generalizability is partly assessable from population, site/operator, and follow-up descriptions, but the SAP/protocol does not fully translate those design constraints into limits on the intended-use claim.

# BIOSTAT-002 Primary endpoint definition

## Question

Is the primary endpoint objectively defined, clinically meaningful, measurable, and aligned with the device's intended purpose?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-002 Primary endpoint definition]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The primary endpoint is time in target range, calculated by pooling CGM readings over the treatment period.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-002-1: DAN06 Study is supported for primary endpoint definition because endpoint clinical justification or measurement validity may need more support

**Trusted source evidence:** ich-e-9-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-step-5_en.pdf, lines 300-323. Semantic confidence: High.

> There should be sufficient evidence that the primary variable can provide a valid and reliable measure of some clinically relevant and important treatment benefit.

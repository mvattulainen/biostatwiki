# BIOSTAT-017 Diagnostic performance

## Question

For diagnostic devices, are sensitivity, specificity, PPV, NPV, ROC/AUC, agreement, indeterminate results, and reference-standard uncertainty handled appropriately?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-017 Diagnostic performance]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The SAP concerns therapeutic glucose-control outcomes, not diagnostic performance.

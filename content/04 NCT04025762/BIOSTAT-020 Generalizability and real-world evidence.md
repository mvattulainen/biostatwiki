# BIOSTAT-020 Generalizability and real-world evidence

## Question

Do the investigation population, sites, operators, follow-up, and real-world data sources support generalization to the intended users, patients, and use environment?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-020 Generalizability and real-world evidence]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The study is multicenter, free-living, and enrolls older adults with type 1 diabetes; generalizability is not fully justified.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-020-1: DAN06 Study is partially supported for generalizability and real-world evidence because generalizability is only partially justified

**Trusted source evidence:** ich-e-9-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-step-5_en.pdf, lines 668-699. Semantic confidence: High.

> Multicentre trials can provide a better basis for generalisation by recruiting from a wider population and broader clinical settings, but implementation should be clear and similar at all centres.

**SAP evidence:** SAP_001.pdf, lines 57-66 and 415-426. Semantic confidence: Medium.

> The study is multicenter, free-living, and enrolls older adults with type 1 diabetes; generalizability is not fully justified.

**Status:** Partially supported

### Gap statement

Complete SAP review shows the target population and setting for DAN06: older adults with type 1 diabetes aged over 60 years who complete run-in competency/compliance requirements; approximately 36 randomized subjects. Generalizability is partly assessable from population, site/operator, and follow-up descriptions, but the SAP/protocol does not fully translate those design constraints into limits on the intended-use claim.

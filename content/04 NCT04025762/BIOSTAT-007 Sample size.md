# BIOSTAT-007 Sample size

## Question

Is the sample size sufficient for the primary effectiveness endpoint, safety endpoint, and any key precision requirements?

Question reference: [[Questions#BIOSTAT-007 Sample size]]

## Short SAP Evidence

The sample size assumes 36 randomized subjects, 80% power, 10% treatment effect, and 18% SD.

## Statistical Claim-to-Evidence

### Claim BIOSTAT-007-1: DAN06 Study is partially supported for sample size because safety precision or assumption basis may be incomplete

**Trusted source evidence:** ich-e-9-statistical-principles-clinical-trials-step-5_en.pdf, lines 878-899. Semantic confidence: High.

> The number of subjects in a clinical trial should always be large enough to provide a reliable answer to the questions addressed. The method by which sample size is calculated should be given with estimates and the basis of those estimates.

**SAP evidence:** SAP_001.pdf, lines 73-76. Semantic confidence: Medium.

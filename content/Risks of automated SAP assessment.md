
Risks:

LLMs are known to hallucinate. That is, present confident assessment with subtle and not so subtle errors. The risk is that automated assessment is falsely understood as complete and correct. As mitigation the assessment should be understood as outcome of a junior statistician needing further review.

The current assessment scheme is also incomplete and leaves out at least two major parts of SAP assessment:
- Assessment of the statistical methods (methods, method assumptions)
- Assessment of implementability of the SAP. The SAP should be detailed enough so that any professional statistical programmer would be able to complete statistical programming without additional information
